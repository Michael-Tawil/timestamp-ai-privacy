Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 24, 2026

Overview

This privacy policy covers all Chrome extensions published by this developer, including TimeStamp AI and SwissKnife AI.

Data Collection

None of our extensions collect, store, transmit or share any personal data whatsoever. All extensions operate entirely on your local device.

What the Extensions Do

TimeStamp AI reads the current date and time from your browser and prepends it to messages you send on ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. SwissKnife AI does the same and additionally allows users to set writing preferences that are injected into AI conversations. All processing happens entirely on your device. No data is sent to any external server.

Permissions

The storage permission is used solely to save your preferences locally in your browser — including toggle state, writing rules and conversation history. The tabs permission is used solely to identify the active tab so rules can be injected into the correct conversation. All data stays on your device and never leaves it.

Third Parties

We do not sell, share or transfer any user data to third parties under any circumstances.
