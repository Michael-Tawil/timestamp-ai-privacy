Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2, 2026

Overview

TimeStamp AI is a Chrome extension that injects the current date, time and timezone into messages sent to AI chatbots. We take privacy seriously. This policy explains exactly what data we do and do not collect.

Data Collection

TimeStamp AI does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data whatsoever. The extension operates entirely on your local device.

What the Extension Does

The extension reads the current date and time from your browser and prepends it to messages you send on ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. This processing happens entirely on your device. No data is sent to any external server.

Permissions

The extension uses the storage permission solely to save your on/off toggle preference locally in your browser. This data never leaves your device.

Third Parties

We do not sell, share or transfer any user data to third parties under any circumstances.
